Species

Each species carries its own strengths, weaknesses, and considerations, don't take the choice lightly. Species perks are granted automatically at enlistment and appear on your sheet.

Humans Sol System · Earth

Humanity's adaptability is second to none. Individual galactic species may hold an edge in a few attributes, but that usually comes at the cost of others. Humans are consistent across the board, making them some of the most well-rounded fighters and operatives around.

Adaptable: Two Attribute boons. You may select two attributes and increase them by +10 each. You cannot pick the same attribute twice. (You can find these in the Items tab, in the Boons folder.)

One Face Among Many: Humans blend in where humans live. Your concealment thresholds are 10% higher in the slums and in megacities.

Paraphysics: Humans are not born to the gift. Awakening takes 2 psycrystals. An awakened Human casts on Charisma and can hold up to 5 disciplines.

Mutant (Baron) Sol System · Earth

A Baron is the rare mutant who came through transformation with their human silhouette and most of their old mind intact. Where a Vassal cannot set foot in a settlement without drawing weapons, a Baron can walk through a market, sit across a negotiating table, or take a contract, their nature passing unnoticed by all but the most careful eye. This makes them their tribe's emissaries to the human world, and it is exactly what makes them suited to a resistance cell.

What brings a Baron to the fight varies. Some are emissaries whose trade routes turned into alliances. Some are mercenaries who took a job and stayed. Some are climbing toward Monarchhood and see GENESIS as the surest proving ground. Barons are the only playable mutant caste.

Base Health
10
Base Energy
6
Species Perks

Controlled Mutations: Roll twice on the Positive Mutation Table and once on the Negative Mutation Table at character creation. Because a Baron's mutations are bestowed under a Monarch's careful guidance, you may reroll the Negative result once and take the second outcome. All results are permanent. If a mutation grants a skill you already have, increase that skill by one rank instead. (See the Mutation Tables page.)

Human Passing: A Baron reads as an ordinary (if slightly off-putting) human under normal observation. Gain an Assistance die on Deception and disguise checks to maintain a human cover, and move through human settlements and most GENESIS checkpoints without automatic suspicion. Openly using overt mutations (wings, bared natural weapons, acid spit, and the like) immediately blows this cover to any human witnesses.

Humanoid Frame: Unlike Vassals, a Baron's build lets them wear manufactured armor and use standard gear normally, with no special modification or cost penalty.

Murk-Eye: A mutant's eyes have a special adaptation to poor light. They suffer no D.M. penalty or skill check penalty due to low light conditions, and can see up to 14 tiles of darkness as if it is standard Night Vision. Complete darkness can still apply penalties to checks.

Latent Claws: A Baron can call forth natural weapons at will: 1d6 + AGI or STR modifier damage, Light quality, undisarmable. Doing so in the open marks them as a mutant (see Human Passing).

Telestoi Eridani System · Elysthae

A proud and magnetic people, who won the Ascension Wars on their home world 5000 years ago. Victory made them complacent and softened their edges. Now, having lost that home to the Overlords, they have traveled through the Stellar Doors. One such door led to Earth, a place where they will learn to fight once more.

The nearest kin to humans of any species, they stand from 5'2" to 6'6". Their skin runs a stone gradient from marble white through gray to onyx, their eyes gold, blue, purple, red or green, and their pointed ears move with their mood.

Base Health
7
Base Energy
11
Species Perks

Telestoian Glamor: You impose a chosen self-image on the minds around you: a disguise as a humanoid near your own build. It cannot make you invisible, or show something far from your true shape. It holds as long as you are conscious and keep 3 Energy committed to it: while it holds, those 3 cannot be spent, and it falls if you have fewer than 3 left. Disorientated does not break it. It is a paraphysical effect on minds, so it does nothing to mind-immune creatures, and cameras and recordings show your true face. Other means can see through it, as the GM rules: a Paraphysics check to notice an anomaly, or another's paraphysical senses. Use this ability to raise the Glamor, and again to let it fall.

Memory Sharing: Out of combat, with a willing creature you touch, you give or receive one memory, lived as if it were their own: a face, a route walked, a conversation, what happened in a room.

Empath: You feel the emotions around you, and can lean on them. Once per session, choose one: add Assistance dice equal to your Paraphysics skill rank to a Persuasion or Insight check, or grant an ally -20 D.M. on their check to break out of the Panicked state.

Gifted Mind: Your people's gift runs deeper than most. You start with rank 1 in Optics and in Telepathy, and in those two disciplines your rank can reach 6 where others stop at 5. Both count toward the three disciplines a Telestoi can hold, so your third is yours to choose.

Telesma: The bioluminescent organ that wraps your head shows what you are: bright in youth and dim with age, cool colors when you are well and hot ones when you are hurt or sick. It pulses when you use paraphysics: casting a power ends any Concealment you have. Your Glamor holds through it.

Paraphysics: Telestoi are born to the gift. They cast on Charisma and hold 3 disciplines. No Telestoi gift works on a mind-immune creature.

Telestoi Backgrounds

House Scion

Born to the noble family of a Telestoi city-state. You were raised on debate, sport and art, the arenas where your people settled every quarrel for five thousand years instead of going to war. You know how to win without a weapon.

Skills: Persuasion, Insight, Deception, History, Tactics, Awareness

Settled by Contest: Once per session, you can challenge a willing rival who is not GENESIS to settle a dispute by contest instead of violence: debate, sport or art, at the GM's check. Win, and they honor the terms.

Court Sense: Once per session, ask the GM who truly holds power in a room or a group, and what they want.

Envoy: When a faction task you worked on succeeds, or partly succeeds, that faction's standing rises by 2 extra points.

Door Warden

Raised on the temporary homeworld after the Exodus, in the service that keeps the Stellar Doors and the drones that watched Earth for a quarter century. You came through the Earth Door knowing this planet's last twenty five years better than most of the people living on it.

Skills: Technology, Xenotechnology, History, Investigation, Awareness, Survivalism

The Watchers' Record: Once per session, ask the GM one thing about a place's history since the invasion: who held it, what happened there, or who passed through.

Stellar Cartographer: Once per mission, you plot the squad's route through a region so that it avoids one GENESIS patrol or one hazard of your choice.

Silent Watchers: When you are assigned to an Intelligence Action, you count Investigation and Awareness at +10 per skill rank instead of +5.

Reticulons Reti System · Reti VI

Brilliant, frail and paraphysically strong. Survivors of when the Overlords stole their shorelines, they spent sixty years adrift in the Ithelû flotilla. They now come to Earth looking for allies, and perhaps a new home.

Reti VI pulls only 0.7 Earth gravity, so their muscles are weaker than a human's and their bones are hollow, like a bird's: a help in the water, a frailty on land. They are lithe and wiry, with sleek, faintly wet skin in greens, teals, blues and tans, an elongated head with fin-like frills, and a large pair of eyes with a smaller second pair above them.

Base Health

Base Energy

Species Perks

Brilliance: Reticulons utilize Intelligence for the casting of any paraphysics.

Freshwater Gills: Gills at your ribcage, the inheritance of an amphibious people. You breathe fresh water as easily as air, and swim at your Run speed. Salt water you cannot breathe: in it you hold your breath like anyone else.

Four Eyes: Your second, smaller pair of eyes sits a little above the first, made for seeing underwater. Smoke, fog, murky water and dim light never hide a target from you within 8 tiles.

Scientists by Nature: Humans are explorers by nature; Reticulons are scientists by nature. When you are assigned to a Research project, you count Technology and Xenotechnology at +10 per skill rank instead of +5.

Paraphysics: Reticulons are born to the gift and hold 4 disciplines. On average they are stronger in the gift than the Telestoi, though the Telestoi reach deeper in theirs.

Reticulon Backgrounds

The Enduring Calm

An initiate of the Ishelûvae, the order the old Vaeshelûn monks became in the flight. You were trained the way your people learned they must be: martial discipline first, because paraphysics used wrongly is devastating. You will fight, but you will not ruin a living place for nothing.

Skills: Paraphysics, Athletics, Acrobatics, Insight, Tactics, Medical

Still Water: The order teaches the gift's dangers before its uses. Once per mission, reroll a failed Paraphysics improvisation check, and a Defeat on it counts as a plain failure.

Tidecraft: Out of combat, once per mission, you shape a body of water you can see, up to the size of a room: part it, still it, or draw it into a wall or a bridge for a few minutes.

Calm Under Pressure: When you are assigned to an Intelligence Action, you count Insight and Paraphysics at +10 per skill rank instead of +5.

Flotilla Born

Sixty years between the stars, running from system to system ahead of the Xargryr. You grew up aboard the Ithelû flotilla: patching ships that should have died, staying silent, and listening to enemy traffic. That is how your people found Earth.

Skills: Technology, Mechanics, Xenotechnology, Hacking, Stealth, Survivalism

Patch It Till It Flies: Once per mission, outside combat, you get one broken machine working again for the rest of the scene: a vehicle, a door mechanism, a generator, a radio. It fails for good afterward.

Signal Intercept: Once per mission, you listen in on Xargryr or GENESIS traffic nearby, and the GM tells you one thing it says: a patrol route, a reinforcement time, or an order.

Shipwright: When you are assigned to an Engineering project, you count Mechanics and Xenotechnology at +10 per skill rank instead of +5.

Khaldar Tau Ceti System · Töörzam

A stout and incredibly hardy race, perhaps one of the toughest within the local cluster, and brilliant material scientists. They stand 3'5" to 4'10", dense under thick overlapping plates of keratin, and sense heat through the stone around them.

Base Health
10
Base Energy
7
Species Perks

Rock and Roll: When you sprint you curl into a ball and roll to your destination. Reaction fire triggered by your movement takes an additional +5. Until the end of your next turn, you gain +1 DR for every 4 tiles rolled. This DR applies to all damage types except Psychic and Cosmic.

Through the Fire and Flames: Your kind has lived in some of the hottest caves and locations, and thanks to this you are particularly hardy against fire. You gain +2 Fire DR innate to your species, and this cannot be shredded.

Crystalwright: You gain 1 Assistance die on checks to identify, assess, repair, modify, or salvage materials, gear, and structures, including crystal tech and xenotech. You can also repair armor mid battle: once per scene, spend an action to restore 1 point of shredded DR on yourself or an adjacent ally.

Stone Gut: You can draw sustenance from minerals and never need rations where stone is available. You gain -10 D.M. on tests to resist ingested poisons and disease.

Paraphysically Null: The Khaldar cannot harness paraphysics. They cannot become paraphysically enabled by any means, including Awakening. A Khaldar may still train the Paraphysics skill for identification and knowledge.

Khaldar Backgrounds

Heat Caller

You were raised among the Khalgir, and the stone speaks to you more clearly than it does to most. Your clan trusted you with their lives, because you told them when to run. You still feel it when a place is about to turn on you.

Skills: Survivalism, Awareness, Investigation, Insight, History, Stealth

Feel the Stone: Once per mission, you read the heat through a wall or floor. The GM tells you how many living creatures and running machines are within 10 tiles, and roughly where.

The Long Warning: Once per mission, you feel the response before it arrives. The GM tells you how long until GENESIS reinforcements reach the squad, and from which direction.

Nothing Left Behind: Once per strategy layer turn, after a successful mission, you bring back an additional 5 Logistics worth of supplies.

Crystal Smith

You learned the craft at the clan forges, where the crystals are handled with reverence and a mistake is a shame carried for life. Everything you make is built to be carried, repaired, and carried again. Now you put that craft to work against the people who took your world.

Skills: Mechanics, Technology, Xenotechnology, Paraphysics, Investigation, Athletics

Reading the Grain: Once per session, you study a piece of gear, a material or a crystal, and the GM tells you what it is, what it was made for, and one thing wrong with it.

Field Forge: Once per mission, out of combat, you rework one ally's weapon or armor. Until the mission ends, a weapon deals +1 damage or a suit of armor gains +1 DR to one damage type.

Material Scientist: When you are assigned to a Research project, you count Mechanics and Xenotechnology at +10 per skill rank instead of +5.
