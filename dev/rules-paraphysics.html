SEC-18.A // Paraphysics

Paraphysics

How a paraphysical operative is built, advanced, and limited. Tactical casting uses the standard d100 roll under chassis. The Paraphysics skill handles the narrative half.

The Three Currencies

Paraphysical advancement runs on three separate currencies. Each does exactly one job, and none of them substitutes for another.

Currencies
CurrencyControlsUsed To
PsycrystalsAccessUnlock a power at Rank 1. Unlock a power's Rank 5 evolution. Enable paraphysics on a character who did not begin with it (2 crystals).
ExperiencePotency and capacityBuy discipline ranks. Buy power ranks 2, 3 and 4. Buy the Paraphysics skill.
EnergyUse, per missionSpent to cast every power and on improvised paraphysics. Shared with role abilities and melee special strikes.

Psycrystals are loot: found, bought and sold like gear, never a standard currency.

Becoming Paraphysically Enabled

Paraphysics is not open to every operative by default. A character must either be born to it or be forcibly opened to it.

Route One: Creation

A character may begin play paraphysically enabled through their species or their background. Telestoi and Reticulons are born to it, and the Awakened background is open to Humans and Mutants. Khaldar cannot become paraphysically enabled by any means. A paraphysical recruit starts with rank 1 in a discipline and one power at Rank 1, both free. A second rank costs 75 training XP.

Route Two: Awakening

A character who did not begin enabled may be opened to paraphysics in play at a cost of 2 Psycrystals. This is a one time expenditure. Awakening can be taught, but the price stays two crystals whoever does the teaching.

  • The character becomes able to buy and use paraphysics permanently.
  • They gain 1 rank in a discipline of their choice.
  • They may learn one power within that discipline, at Rank 1.

The granted rank opens two power slots. The granted power fills one, and the other waits for a psycrystal. From that point forward they advance on the same tracks as anyone else.

Humans cast on Charisma because paraphysics is so new to them that using it is a battle of presence, not knowledge. Telestoi cast on Charisma as a naturally charismatic and emotional people. Reticulons cast on Intelligence: they have lived with it and used it the longest.

SEC-18.B // Paraphysics

Disciplines

A discipline is a school of paraphysical practice and functions as a miniature class in its own right. Ranks are bought with XP alone and cap at Rank 5. Species bonuses may raise an individual cap above 5.

What Discipline Rank Does

The Three Benefits of Discipline Rank
BenefitEffect
AccessGates which powers in that discipline may be unlocked. A power of tier N requires discipline rank N.
CapacityTwo powers per rank held in that discipline. Capacity is per discipline and never pools across schools.
EfficiencyReduces the Energy cost of the powers in that discipline you have outgrown: -1 for each rank above the power's tier.

Discipline rank contributes nothing to accuracy, potency, damage, duration or range. Those live entirely in power ranks.

Where the Lines Fall

  • The mind: Telepathy acts on cognition and fails entirely against mind immune creatures. Optics acts on perception and does not, so it works on them, and a machine's optical sensor can still be fooled.
  • Movement: Any discipline may grant or deny ordinary movement, never teleportation.
  • Protective cover: Astrokinesis grants mobile soft cover centered on the caster, requiring Concentration, so a flashbang ends it.
  • Squadsight: The squad shares vision, so an Optics reveal is squad wide by default: one action, and every operative can engage that target. Denial is just as severe, removing a target from the whole squad rather than from one shooter.
SEC-18.C // Paraphysics

Powers and Their Ranks

Every power is its own five rank tree. Entry and apex are gated by psycrystals. Everything between is bought with XP on the player's own schedule.

Power Ranks
RankCostWhat It Does
1 Unlock1 CrystalThe power exists and is usable. Requires discipline rank at least equal to the power's tier, and an open slot in that discipline. Nothing stops a caster filling a new slot with a lower tier power.
2 Advancement20 XPThe first step on the power's tracks: Accuracy -10, Potency +4.
3 Choice40 XPSelect one modification from those the power offers. The power's only choice node, and it is permanent.
4 Advancement60 XPThe second and larger step: Accuracy a further -15 (-25 total), Potency a further +6 (+10 total).
5 Evolution1 CrystalThe power reaches its final form, written for that power alone. Where a power offers more than one evolution you pick one, and the choice is permanent.

A full power costs 120 XP and 2 crystals. Each power writes out its own ranks, so its card is the final word on what it gains at each one.

Profile Tracks

Each power advances along two of five tracks, chosen to suit what the power is for. No power advances all five.

The Five Tracks
TrackWhat It Moves
AccuracyAttack D.M. on the d100 casting roll. -10 at Rank 2, -25 total at Rank 4.
PotencyD.M. imposed on the target's saving throw. +4 at Rank 2, +10 total at Rank 4.
DamageDice or flat bonus on damaging powers.
DurationRounds an effect persists, where applicable.
RangeOptimal range band in tiles.

SEC-18.D // Paraphysics

Resolution in Combat

Tactical casting uses the standard d100 roll under chassis. Nothing here is a separate subsystem.

Attack Powers

Roll 1d100 and apply modifiers. The result must land at or under the caster's casting attribute.

Modifiers on a Paraphysical Attack
SourceValue
Power rank (Accuracy track)-10 at Rank 2, -25 at Rank 4
In Optimal Range2x casting modifier
Out of Optimal Range+15. Optimal range is set per power.
Paraphysical focus-20, sharing the -25 ceiling with the Accuracy track
Cover+30 half, +45 full
Target armor DefenseAs armor
Example // Gravbolt

Ragnar casts Gravbolt (Rank 4, Accuracy track at -25) against a GENESIS trooper in half cover, In Optimal Range. His Charisma is 55, so his modifier is 5.

1d100 - 25 (Power Rank) - 10 (2x CHA, IOR) + 6 (Trooper Armor) + 30 (Half Cover)
Rolls 46 // 46 - 35 + 36 = 47

47 is at or under 55. Gravbolt hits. A focus would add nothing here: at Rank 4 the Accuracy track already fills the -25 ceiling.

The Paraphysical Focus

A focus gives -20 on attack powers, but the focus and the Accuracy track together never exceed -25. It carries a fledgling power and is redundant for a power at Rank 4. Because the ceiling is per power, a caster with several low rank powers keeps wanting one.

The focus counts against carry capacity, cannot be concealed, and is not expected to take mods. A low rank caster on an infiltration mission chooses between the focus and getting through the checkpoint.

Saving Throw Powers

The target rolls 1d100 and must land at or under the attribute the power tests. The caster adds to that roll:

Save D.M. = casting modifier + Potency track (+4 at Rank 2, +10 at Rank 4)

Damage and Defenses

  • Most paraphysical attacks bypass the Ablative Armor pool. Damage goes to barriers first, then to HP.
  • Most paraphysics deal non standard damage types (Cold, Electric, Psychic and so on). The target consults their DR table. Psychic and Cosmic DR start at 0.
  • Paraphysics are unaffected by Suppressing Fire.

Concentration

A caster can hold Concentration on one power at a time, and concentrating on a new power lets the old one go. The Disorientated condition breaks it, making a flashbang a cheap and universally available counter. Every power that requires Concentration says so on its card. An area centered on the caster is drawn around their token and lasts as long as the power does.

Barriers

A paraphysical barrier shares one pool with armor barriers, needs no armor to hold, and never stacks: it lifts your Barrier to its value rather than adding on top.

SEC-18.E // Paraphysics

Energy

Paraphysics draws on the same Energy pool as role abilities, melee special strikes and chemthrower bursts. Your pool is your species' Base Energy plus your Charisma bonus plus your Intelligence bonus, and perks can raise it substantially.

The Discipline Discount

  • -1 Energy for each discipline rank above the power's tier. A power costs its listed price at the rank that unlocks it and gets cheaper only as you outgrow it. A tier 5 power is never discounted.
  • Applies only to powers with a base cost of 4 or more.
  • Hard floor: half base cost, rounded up, across all sources combined. This covers the Efficient modification as well, so a 5 cost power never drops below 3 regardless of how many reductions stack.

SEC-18.F // Paraphysics

The Paraphysics Skill

The Paraphysics skill is a normal DYNOSRS skill bought with XP. It governs the narrative half of paraphysics and never touches tactical casting accuracy or potency.

What the Skill Covers
UseRequires Discipline Ranks?
Identifying paraphysical anomalies in the fieldNo
Knowledge of paraphysics, practitioners and phenomenaNo
Improvising effects outside combat where no explicit power covers the attemptYes

The first two uses are open to anyone who trains the skill, including characters who are not paraphysically enabled.

Improvised Paraphysics

A character with ranks in a discipline may attempt effects within that discipline that no power of theirs covers.

  • Out of combat only, meaning outside initiative. There is no in combat exception.
  • The effect must sit within a discipline the character holds ranks in. No improvising outside your schools.
  • Difficulty scales with reach. An effect within the character's discipline rank is a standard check. One tier beyond is Hard. Two tiers beyond is Formidable. Three or more is outside what the character can attempt.
  • It costs Energy, set by the GM against the same bands as powers, sized to the effect attempted.
  • If a power covers it, you need the power. Improvisation produces effects that powers do not. It does not produce cheaper versions of effects that powers do.
Reading a Paraphysics Improvisation Roll
ResultOutcome
Success, cleanThe effect occurs as described.
Success with DangerThe effect occurs with a complication: additional Energy drawn, attention attracted, an unintended secondary consequence, or a lingering disturbance at the site.
FailureThe effect does not occur. Energy is still spent.
DefeatParaphysical mishap. The GM may invert the intended effect, inflict a condition on the caster, or let the attempt reach something that should have stayed unreached.
