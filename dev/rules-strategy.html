SEC-19.A // Strategy Layer

The Strategy Layer

The rebellion against the invaders is a world war unlike any other. Countless resistance groups across the globe fight small battles with GENESIS, but without support and greater coordination these efforts will prove futile. The ODR can fill this need, and in turn these resistance groups are willing to pledge their support in the fight against GENESIS.

It is this combined effort that gives the fight to retake Earth any chance of success. The ODR, for all their knowledge and superior equipment, are far too few in number to hold any liberated territory. The Long War can only be won when soldiers and civilians alike are willing to throw off their chains and take a stand for what they believe in.

What is the Strategy Layer?

The strategy layer is meant to capture the feeling of being in a war room, looking over maps and formulating the overarching plans that turn the effort to liberate Earth from a vague ambition into a defined and actionable strategy.

This creates narratives and a greater intent behind tactical actions than simply killing all the enemies on the map. Consider two squads:

  • Squad A is trying to keep a low profile while collecting information on a major GENESIS asset to sabotage.
  • Squad B is gathering supplies and materials in a smash-and-grab raid before reinforcements show up.

The targets chosen, the equipment loadout taken, and the tactical approaches used will vary significantly between them.

These tactical actions then filter up into the strategy layer. They provide useful information and material while drawing GENESIS attention away from important infrastructure, which can then be destroyed to soften up Megacities for assault.

The Long War cannot be won solely through force of arms. A keen mind that can understand the enemy, their weaknesses, and how to best exploit them can accomplish far more than brute force ever could.

Retaking Earth

The struggle to retake Earth is a truly massive endeavor. To decisively defeat GENESIS, all Megacities in an area must be successfully taken over. For the purposes of our playtest, Neo Seattle will be the goal.

Megacity Objectives

Every Megacity has three assets that must be taken:

  1. A military citadel
  2. A motor pool
  3. A center of government

Once all three are captured, GENESIS' ability to contest the Resistance is effectively shattered in that area. The Megacity and surrounding regions are considered liberated.

Liberated Region Bonuses

  • Each Megacity has a unique landmark that can provide bonuses.
  • Liberated Megacities count as ODR bases for any Strategy Layer action.
  • Negative events in a liberated region are automatically handled by Resistance forces and don't require ODR attention, unless stated otherwise.
  • GENESIS ground forces cannot move through a liberated region unless a Reprisal mission is successful there first.

Campaign Scope

For the moment, only the West Coast of the United States is fully mapped out, and we consider the typical campaign to be similar in scope. However, because these systems are designed to be scalable, you could run an entire campaign in a smaller area like a single Megacity, or scale it up to require liberating the entire world.

Numbers in These Pages

Every number the turn engine uses is a default the GM can change in Configure Settings, under Strategy Layer. These pages show the defaults.

SEC-19.B // Strategy Layer

GENESIS Regions

GENESIS has nominal control over Earth. This control is broken down into regions, each with two statistics: Strength and Heat.

Strength

Strength represents the concentration of power in a region, from 0 to 25. Higher values allow for denser deployments of higher quality units.

  • Low Strength: patrols of a few troopers.
  • High Strength: elite Xar'Nath squads with paraphysical specialists and close air support.

Regions that GENESIS projects power from will have high Strength, so direct attacks against them are often suicidal. However, the ODR can exploit the sluggish and reactive nature of GENESIS. By undertaking operations in other regions to either bolster the resistance or subvert the aliens, GENESIS is forced to react. Strength will be redirected to these other regions, allowing convoys of soldiers to be ambushed and leaving the vaunted cities and fortresses of GENESIS open to attack.

Heat

Heat is a measure of how alert the GENESIS forces in a region are. There are five Heat levels:

Heat Levels
LevelExample Response
NonePolice response before military units are mobilized.
LowMilitary units are mobilized first, in ground transports.
MediumAlien and GENESIS units arrive by air and ground at greater speed. Stratagems and combat vehicles start to be deployed.
HighElite soldiers drop on the squad's position from orbit within minutes.
MaximumAlien combat units are teleported to the squad's position.

Heat is the primary statistic GENESIS considers when moving forces on the strategy layer, and it controls how quickly and aggressively they respond to resistance activity.

Note

It might be tempting to ignore locations with high Strength or Heat and focus solely on low-risk areas. However, some research and upgrades can only be unlocked by fighting enemies and salvaging equipment that only consistently deploy at higher Heat or Strength levels.

Raising and Lowering Heat

GENESIS is a highly reactive force.

  • Heat increases as GENESIS soldiers and assets are attacked. More important assets, like military bases or propaganda centers, increase Heat by far more than a few skirmishes. This can be a great way to draw attention away from your real target while getting some good equipment in the process.
  • Heat decreases by 1 level in regions where no tactical actions or relevant events have taken place for two turns.
  • Increases or decreases in Heat take effect at the beginning of the next strategy layer turn.

SEC-19.C // Strategy Layer

The Resistance

The struggle against GENESIS is an uphill battle, but properly supplied and supported, the Resistance can prove to be stalwart and capable fighters. The Resistance's ability to combat the invaders is measured by Logistics and Reputation.

  • Logistics covers all forms of supply and material concerns, from food stores to ammunition and medicine.
  • Reputation governs the popularity of the Resistance among the general population, as well as their willingness to rise up and fight their alien overlords.

Keeping the Resistance supplied and liked by the people is essential for long-term success, as the ODR cannot hold the vast swathes of liberated territory by themselves.

Logistics

Logistics is the currency of the strategy layer and encompasses all forms of goods that can be used directly or traded for something that can be.

Gaining Logistics

  • Performing missions in a region
  • Certain event cards

Spending Logistics

  • Support in tactical battles (see below)
  • Researching new equipment
  • Building regional upgrades

Losing Logistics

  • Certain event cards
  • Successful GENESIS Reprisals

Logistics are meant to be used, not hoarded, as equipment is far more expendable than a skilled soldier.

General Uses of Logistics
SupportCostEffect

Reputation

It's been true across time that no insurgency can succeed without the support of the people, and here it is no different. The Resistance's Reputation in a region approximates how popular and supported the Resistance is by the general populace.

Reputation runs on nine levels: Hostile, Resentful, Wary, Uneasy, Neutral, Sympathetic, Friendly, Supportive, Devoted. Each level holds a 10 point bar. When the bar fills the level rises, and when it empties the level falls. Either way the new level starts at 5 and any overflow is thrown away, so Reputation is hard to move and easy to hold.

Effects of Reputation

  • High Reputation (Friendly and above): Civilians might look the other way, or even stick their necks out for the ODR of their own will, potentially buying just enough time for the squad to escape.
  • Low Reputation (Wary and below): People might call GENESIS if the squad is spotted, or even attack them to curry favor with the aliens.

Reputation can often be the intangible edge the squad needs to get away, as even the smallest help at the right time can be the difference between life and death.

Factions

Standing with each major and minor faction runs on the same nine level bar as Reputation, from At War, Hostile, Unfriendly, Wary, Neutral, Cordial, Friendly, Trusted, Allied. Factions offer tasks each turn, and players send their own operatives to work them. A task done earns +2 standing with its faction, and a task failed costs -2.

Intel

Intel is an uncommon secondary resource the Resistance uses to locate GENESIS assets or specific resources like exotic Research Salvage.

  • Obtained only through: breaching GENESIS security, interrogating VIPs, or other forms of espionage.
  • Used to: boost Intelligence Actions and execute more exotic and powerful stratagems.
SEC-19.D // Strategy Layer

Strategy Turns

Each strategy layer turn represents one week of in-universe time and runs through three phases: GENESIS Phase, Event Phase, ODR Phase, then repeat.

GENESIS Phase

Order of operations:

  1. Every 8 turns: Each Megacity region gains +1 Strength.
  2. Strength moves: GENESIS moves Strength to adjacent regions with a higher Heat value, preferring to transfer Strength from rural and wildland regions first, then slums, then Megacities. A region pulls 3 Strength from one cooler neighbor each turn.
  3. Heat adjusts: Heat values increase or decrease based on regional activity.
  4. Every 4 turns: The GM draws 3 Dark Event cards and picks 2 to use. Dark Event cards must be used in the same turn they are drawn unless the card indicates otherwise.
  5. Discretionary cards: The GM may play any discretionary Dark Event cards. Only one Reprisal card can be used at a time if multiple are available.

A liberated region sits out the GENESIS Phase.

Event Phase

The Event Phase occurs every 2nd turn. Order of operations:

  1. Clear old events: If persistent event cards are in play, discard them and apply their discard effects, if any.
  2. Draw: The GM draws 3 Event cards face down, and 1 World Event.
  3. Distribute: Place the cards at random across the regions, no more than one card per region.
  4. Reveal all cards.
  5. Apply effects. Instantaneous cards apply immediately and are then discarded. Persistent cards (effects that can be interacted with or countered) stay on the region until the end of the next strategy layer turn, then are discarded.
Note

Some cards have effects that only activate when discarded, or amplify their effects when discarded.

ODR Phase

The ODR Phase is the player phase and the most involved. Actions are taken in this order:

  1. Research Actions
  2. Engineering Actions
  3. Intelligence Actions
  4. Tactical Actions

Once all actions are taken, the current strategy layer turn ends and the cycle begins anew.

SEC-19.E // Strategy Layer

Research Actions

Research covers all forms of investigation into materials, both mundane and alien, and how they can be applied to bolster the ODR's effort to retake the planet.

Research typically doesn't provide benefits on its own. Instead, it unlocks equipment or unique services that ODR operatives can purchase at will, like better weaponry or genetic modifications. While it might not provide immediate benefits, research is essential to the war effort, as it will even the playing field and allow the ODR to step out of the shadows and contest the aliens directly.

Rules

  • Only one research project can be undertaken at a time.
  • You can switch to a different project at any point with no penalty.

Progressing Research

  • Passive progress: 10 Logistics per strategy layer turn.
  • Spending Logistics: The intended way to complete research quickly.
  • Assigned soldiers: Each soldier contributes additional Logistics per turn equal to their Intelligence bonus + 5 per rank in the relevant skill. The soldier must have one of the project's Specialization skills. A soldier can only contribute using one skill, even if they have several qualifying skills, and can only be assigned to one project at a time.
  • Specialized Salvage: Scientists worked fervently during the war to close the technology gap, and GENESIS has plenty of useful resources that can be stolen by an enterprising rebel. Salvage locations can be pinpointed using Long Term Intelligence Actions. Each recovered salvage contributes progress equal to 30% of the project's Logistics cost.

SEC-19.F // Strategy Layer

Engineering Actions

Engineering Actions cover all forms of infrastructure building, as well as the maintenance and deployment of larger assets like vehicles.

Engineering projects typically provide immediate benefits to the region where they were built, affect the ODR as a whole, or provide unique tactical assets that change how squads approach situations.

Rules

  • Up to 3 Engineering Actions can be active at a time, and all active projects progress passively, 10 Logistics per turn.
  • Projects are completed using Logistics.
  • Upgrades built via Engineering Actions can be destroyed in GENESIS Reprisal actions.

SEC-19.G // Strategy Layer

Intelligence Actions

Intelligence Actions cover locating areas rich in resources, or GENESIS assets that need to be destroyed to undermine the aliens' control and draw Strength away from Megacities.

There are two categories:

  • Short Term: Locate specific resources the ODR needs to achieve victory, often in greater quantities than what can be found on most missions.
  • Long Term: Locate major assets, counter Dark Events, or stop a regional crisis.

You may run one Short Term and one Long Term action at the same time.

Progressing Intelligence Actions

  • Passive progress: 10 Intel per strategy layer turn, on each active action. This is progress, not Intel added to your stock.
  • Assigned soldiers: Soldiers with any Social skill, Stealth, Hacking, or Lockpicking each contribute Intel per turn equal to the skill's attribute modifier + 5 per rank in the skill. A perk that counts another skill on Intelligence Actions lets that skill contribute too.
  • Spending Intel: Intel can be spent to progress Intelligence Actions. If things are balanced as intended, it should ideally be spent on Long Term actions to complete them in a reasonable timeframe.

SEC-19.H // Strategy Layer

Tactical Actions

Tactical Actions are the ODR deciding what to actually take action against that turn.

  • One Tactical Action can be taken per strategy layer turn.
  • Tactical Actions are taken at the end of the turn, and completing one advances the strategy layer to the next round.
  • Once a Tactical Action is chosen, a mission is generated for soldiers to deploy on.
  • Some Tactical Actions are always available; others must be revealed by Intelligence Actions.

Always-available Tactical Actions typically reward fewer resources, but provide a consistent flow of material and experience and are the easiest for new characters.

Losing Ground

When a region is liberated, nearby Megacities and their surrounding regions spike in Heat, and Strength begins to concentrate there. Once an adjacent region reaches a certain amount of Strength, GENESIS launches an Incursion to retake the liberated region. This prompts a high-priority mission to kneecap the Incursion so the Resistance can handle the rest.

  • The spike: Regions within 2 borders of the liberated area gain +2 Heat, up to Maximum.
  • The Incursion: It launches when a neighbor of a liberated region reaches 20 Strength.
  • Stopping it: The ODR has 1 turn. A broken Incursion costs the staging region 8 Strength.
  • If it lands: The target becomes Contested, and 8 Strength moves in from the staging region.
